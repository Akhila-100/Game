# Mystery Forest Adventure

A fun, interactive text-based adventure game where you make choices that determine your fate in a mysterious forest!

## Features

- 🎮 **Interactive Gameplay** - Make choices at different points in the story
- 🌲 **Multiple Endings** - Your decisions lead to 4 different endings
- 📝 **Simple to Play** - Easy-to-follow menu system
- 🔄 **Replay Option** - Play again without restarting

## How to Play

1. **Run the game:**
   ```bash
   python mystery_forest_adventure.py
   ```

2. **Enter your name** when prompted

3. **Make choices** by entering the number corresponding to your choice

4. **Reach an ending** based on your decisions

5. **Play again** or exit the game

## Game Endings

The game has 4 different endings:

- 🎉 **Treasure Ending** - Find a hidden treasure and become wealthy
- 💚 **Friendship Ending** - Make friends with a forest guardian
- 😕 **Lost Ending** - Get lost but find safety and help
- 🏃 **Coward Ending** - Walk away from the adventure

## Example Playthrough

```
Enter your name: Sarah

Welcome Sarah!
You are standing at the entrance of a mysterious forest.

What do you want to do?
1. Enter the forest
2. Walk away and go home

Choose (1 or 2): 1

You bravely enter the forest and find two paths.
1. Take the dark path (mysterious)
2. Take the river path (safer)
3. Climb a tree to look around

Choose (1, 2, or 3): 2

[Game continues with your choices...]
```
